## Playback controls

Standard controls such as play, pause, seek and volume are planned. Keyboard shortcuts will be listed here once they are final.

## Accessibility options

Accessibility options will be documented for each platform with the first release. Voice control is intended to help people who prefer not to use a pointer or keyboard, but it will not be the only way to use the player.
