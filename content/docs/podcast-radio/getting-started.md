LSI Podcast and Online Radio is a product concept. Nothing has been released, so this page describes what is intended.

## For listeners

1. Find a show, episode or radio channel.
2. Play it, and use the listening controls to pause or move through it.
3. Follow shows you like.

## For creators

1. Set up a show or a radio channel and add its details.
2. Publish episodes, or schedule or start a live stream.
3. Review how it is performing.

Each step will be documented in full as the capability becomes available.
