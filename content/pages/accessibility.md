LSI Corp wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

## What we have done

- Every control can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
- Status is always written in words and never shown by colour alone.
- Text and controls are designed to meet AA contrast in light and dark colour schemes.
- The site does not play audio or video automatically and does not rely on animation.
- Download buttons name their platform and version.

## Known limitations

This website is an application drawn inside the browser rather than a set of standard web pages. Because of that:

- Screen reader support depends on your browser and may be incomplete.
- Browser features such as find-in-page, text zoom and reader mode may not work as they do on ordinary pages.
- The first visit needs a larger download than a typical web page.

A short plain-text summary of the site is shown if the application cannot start.

## Reporting a problem

If something on this site is not accessible to you, please tell us through [the contact page](/contact).
