This notice covers the LSI Corp website only. Each product in our portfolio has its own privacy information, linked from its page once the product is released.

## What this website collects

- **Browsing the site.** You do not need an account. The website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking, and sets no cookies for visitors.
- **The contact form.** If you send us an enquiry, we receive what you enter: your name, your email address, your organisation if you give one, the service you are asking about and your message. We use it only to reply to you and to handle your enquiry. You are asked to agree to this before sending.
- **Staff sign-in.** If you are a member of LSI staff and sign in, your sign-in session is stored in your browser so that you stay signed in.
- **Technical logs.** The services that host this website and its content may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of request, to keep the service secure and working.

Do not put passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the contact form.

## Who can see an enquiry

Only LSI staff. Enquiries are not published and are not shared with other organisations.

## Asking us to remove your details

Tell us through the [contact page](/contact) and we will delete your enquiry.

## Links to other sites

Download and launch buttons can take you to an app store or to a product's own domain. Those links are labelled, and the destination's own privacy terms apply once you leave.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the review date shown on this page is updated.
